Мисията на JACI

JACI е дигитална екосистема за хората, които създават знания, идеи и реална стойност. Платформата има за цел да направи професионалния и обществения принос по-видим, да насърчава признанието и да създава нови възможности за развитие и сътрудничество.

Our mission is to make contribution visible.

About

Assoc. Prof. Dr. Georgi Nikolov is a researcher and academic leader specializing in regional development, regional management, smart cities, state policies and strategies, regional competitiveness and sustainable territorial development.


He serves as Deputy Dean of the Faculty of Management and Administration and Director of the Institute for Postgraduate Qualification at the University of National and World Economy in Sofia, Bulgaria.

Professional Story

His academic work connects research, teaching and practical engagement with public institutions and local communities. His professional path is focused on creating knowledge that can support better regional policies, smarter cities and stronger territorial development.
